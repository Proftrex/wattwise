WattWise Privacy Policy

Understand where your electricity goes.

Last updated: August 12, 2026

1. Information We Collect

WattWise ("we", "our", or "the app") is an electricity tracking application designed to help users understand and monitor their household electricity consumption.

Google Account Information

When you use WattWise with your Google account, the app may process:

  • Your Google account email address
  • Your Google account name

This information is used to identify your account and provide access to the application.

Electricity and Appliance Information

You may provide information such as:

  • Appliance names
  • Appliance categories
  • Appliance power ratings
  • Usage hours
  • Electricity consumption information
  • Electricity rates
  • Electricity bill information
  • Other information you choose to enter

2. How We Use Your Information

WattWise uses collected information to:

  • Provide electricity tracking features
  • Calculate estimated electricity consumption and costs
  • Display appliance and household electricity information
  • Provide electricity bill calculations and summaries
  • Maintain user access to the application
  • Operate and improve the application

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

WattWise uses Google services, including Google Apps Script and Google Sheets, to process and store information required for the application's functionality.

Your information may therefore be stored in or processed through Google's infrastructure.

4. Data Sharing

WattWise does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed through Google services that are necessary to operate the application.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by WattWise. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their WattWise account information and associated application data by contacting the WattWise application developer.

Upon receiving a valid deletion request, we will take reasonable steps to delete the applicable information, subject to information that must be retained for legitimate operational, legal, or security purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

WattWise is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.

9. Contact

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the WattWise application developer.
